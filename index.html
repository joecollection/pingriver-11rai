Data Integrity Flag
Two figures in the seller's data do not fully reconcile: (1) 11-3-49 Rai converts to ~18,996 Sq.m. under standard Rai-Ngan-Wah conversion, versus the 18,196 Sq.m. stated — a ~4% variance; and (2) ฿425,000,000 ÷ 4,549 Sq.Wah (based on the stated 18,196 Sq.m.) computes to ~฿93,428/Sq.Wah, not the quoted ฿100,000/Sq.Wah. Neither discrepancy is disqualifying, but both should be resolved via a formal land survey before final underwriting.
